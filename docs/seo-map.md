## Rules

- Never claim "first", "only", "best" or any stat we can't prove (CLAUDE.md §2, §4).
- One `<h1>` per page, question-style `<h2>`s where natural, FAQ on every offer page (feeds `FAQPage` schema).
- Meta descriptions 140 to 160 characters, unique per page, written for the click, with the primary term early.
- No em or en dashes in any copy (`scripts/check-dashes.mjs`).
